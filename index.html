About

I am a physics PhD candidate at the University of Houston. My research combines experimental data analysis, relativistic hydrodynamics, and thermodynamics to study matter produced in high-energy nuclear collisions.

Before graduate school, I earned a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Houston. I am interested in problems at the intersection of physics, computation, instrumentation, and energy technology.

02

Research

STAR Collaboration · Heavy-Ion Physics

Extracting the speed of sound in nuclear matter

My current work measures the speed of sound in matter created in ultra-central Au+Au collisions across RHIC beam energies. The analysis uses the logarithmic relationship between mean transverse momentum and charged-particle multiplicity to constrain the equation of state.
